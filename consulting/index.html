The idea

What this practice will offer

The consulting industry is being restructured by AI. Large firms are slow to adopt and expensive by design. Solo practitioners with deep domain expertise and AI in the analytical workflow can now deliver analysis that previously required a team — faster and at lower cost.

My focus is health and energy policy — two domains where rigorous quantitative analysis is in high demand and where my academic background in applied microeconomics is directly applicable. The methodology combines economic modeling, data analysis, and AI-augmented synthesis to produce findings that traditional firms handle slowly and expensively.

Target clients (planned)

  • Think tanks and policy organizations needing rapid quantitative analysis
  • Nonprofits in health or energy requiring economic modeling or program evaluation
  • Government agencies and contractors with data-intensive research questions
  • Academic research groups needing applied empirical work
