About me

DE

I'm a Ph.D. student in economics at Johns Hopkins University, where I focus on applied microeconomics. Before graduate school I worked as an economics instructor and tutor, helping students from middle school through college level navigate quantitative coursework.

I've tutored over a hundred students across math, statistics, and economics — including AP exam preparation, undergraduate coursework, and GRE/GMAT quantitative sections. My approach combines strong mathematical foundations with clear explanations and original materials produced in LaTeX/Overleaf, so students get problem sets and practice exams tailored to their exact course.

Every session is recorded and shared with parents. I believe transparency is the most important thing a private tutor can offer.
