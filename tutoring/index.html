For parents & guardians

Transparency is the standard

Because education is an institution with the development of youth at its core, a serious effort is made so that each tutoring session and the knowledge exchanged is readily available to parents and legal guardians.

What parents receive

  • A signed service agreement before the first session — one page, plain language
  • Zoom recording of every session, shared to a private Dropbox folder within a few hours
  • Brief email summary after each session (or weekly digest for ongoing students)
  • Direct email access to me — parent communication takes priority over student communication
  • Consent documented in writing before any session takes place

Materials policy

  • All practice problems and problem sets are original, produced in LaTeX — not copied from textbooks
  • Student course materials (syllabi, assignments) are used only in-session and not retained
  • No student names, likenesses, or work samples are used in marketing without explicit written consent
  • Student information is stored in a private, password-protected Dropbox folder
