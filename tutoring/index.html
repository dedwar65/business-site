Process

How sessions work

Every session is structured for transparency and effectiveness. Parents of minor students are always kept in the loop.

1

Book & complete intake

Book your free first session and pick a time. You'll receive a short intake form to help me prepare.

2

Confirm the details

A calendar invite arrives right after booking, with a video link for online sessions or the confirmed address for in-person and at-home sessions.

3

Parent visibility

Parents and guardians are welcome to be present during any session. Written consent is required before working with any student under 18.

4

Follow-up summary

After each session you receive a brief summary of what was covered, what to review, and what's coming next.

Where we meet

Session formats

Choose whatever works best for your student. Rates are the same for every format.

Online

Live video with screen sharing and a digital whiteboard. Available anywhere.

In person

At a public location such as a library or study space in the Houston area.

At home

I come to your home anywhere within about 50 miles of Houston. For students under 18, a parent, guardian, or another family member 18 or older must be home for the entire session.
